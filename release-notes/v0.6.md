## November 20, 2026 printing

Start with **Chapter 22, The Shadow Run**. QueueLab simulates an engineering support queue. Each model investigates the same fictional cases before and after fine-tuning, so you can see whether it gets better at checking the records before recommending a fix or handing a case off.

On 60 fictional cases, SmolLM2 went from 0 to 53 supported decisions. Falcon went from 0 to 3 and made more unsupported recommendations, so it did not pass. This printing clarifies how the test worked in Chapter 22 and Appendix F. The results and illustration haven’t changed.

**Appendix F** links to the open-source simulation, measured scorecard, raw traces, and before-and-after replay. These are results from fictional cases, not evidence of customer impact.
