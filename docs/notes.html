Notes for the Second Edition

November 20, 2026 printing

If you already read the Second Edition, start with Chapter 22, The Shadow Run. The new QueueLab case starts with one missing export and tests whether further training changes what a model can justify from the records it reads.

The experiment compared each model with its own unchanged version. On 60 fictional engineering support cases, SmolLM2 went from 0 to 53 supported decisions. Falcon went from 0 to 3 and made more unsupported recommendations, so it did not earn a pass. That difference is worth seeing before you decide what a training score entitles a system to do.

Appendix F points to the open-source companion, the measured scorecard, and the before-and-after replay. The new illustration shows what changed between the runs and what stayed fixed. These are synthetic results from known failure families, not evidence of customer impact.
