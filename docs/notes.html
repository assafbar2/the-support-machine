Notes for the Second Edition

November 20, 2026 printing

[Watch the QueueLab before-and-after replay](https://assafbar2.github.io/queuelab/post-training/). Follow the missing nightly export from Chapter 22, The Shadow Run. See which records each model checked and what it did next. The replay uses saved runs, so there’s nothing to install.

QueueLab simulates an engineering support queue. Each model investigates the same fictional cases before and after fine-tuning. On 60 cases, SmolLM2 went from 0 to 53 supported decisions; Falcon went from 0 to 3 and made more unsupported recommendations, so it did not pass. These are results from fictional cases, not evidence of customer impact.

Chapter 22 now links to the replay before the case begins. Appendix F leads with the replay and points to the source, scorecard, and raw traces. The online book, PDF, DOCX, Markdown, and book skill all carry the same links. The results and illustration haven’t changed, and earlier printings are preserved.
