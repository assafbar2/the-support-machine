For automation = involvement × conditional resolution, show that both factors use the same eligible population, period, and definitions.

For delayed outcomes, count each original conversation once; show 7-day and 30-day cumulative windows separately. Preserve confirmed returns, uncertain matches, independently confirmed outcomes, and insufficient evidence. No observed return is not confirmed success.

For reviewer scores, disclose rubric version, calibration, missing context, baseline versus targeted samples, and disagreement.

For comparisons, record changes in population, case mix, metric definition, product, and grader. Mark breaks in the series; do not label a denominator change an improvement.

For customer signals, distinguish messages, distinct issues, and distinct customers. Show duplicate handling and coverage by channel. Review inferred sentiment against the source exchange before acting. A count of initial human requests does not establish a preference among customers who did not make that request.

Related measures: customer effort, human effort and case mix, missed required escalations, total operating cost, and customer milestones beyond support.
