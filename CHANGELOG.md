# Changelog

## v0.3 — November 1, 2026 — Review edition

Six new chapters expand the book from 23 to 29 chapters. Coordinated updates cover the operating material and eight appendices. See the [reader notes](release-notes/v0.3.md) for where to reread and the [coverage record](release-notes/v0.3-coverage.md) for the full scope.

Added:

- Organizational NIMBY cases, continuing evaluation, and build-versus-buy chapters.
- Customer-agent verification, action receipts, and an accountable recovery playbook.
- Customer promise tracking, mature 7/30-day cohorts, definition-aware metrics, support teams shipping fixes, and a reusable shadow release gate.
- All 29 chapter field notes together with nine dated observations, plus a current vendor snapshot covering Fin, Pylon, Salesforce, and Plain.
- A repository appendix, 80 practical prompts for talking to the book, and research/product sources organized by chapter.
- Eight new templates, revisions to the six existing templates, and a standalone support-ai-evals skill with a tested local cohort calculator.
- Reader notes, all-format downloads, an editions page, and complete review text.
- A visible feature celebrating The Prompt Room podcast with YouTube and Spotify links, plus transcript-informed additions in Chapters 5, 17, 18, 19, and 23.
- Skill installation, verification, and update instructions at the start of Appendix G, with ZIP downloads that contain the revised book.

Refined:

- A personal-authorship note and clearer distinctions among the author's experience, company policy, and recommended practice. Generalized unnecessary employer references, internal operating details, and claims about future plans or guaranteed outcomes.
- A more precise implementation-partner account focused on delivery, ownership, and knowledge transfer, with credit for the vendor's direct technical involvement.
- A concise podcast introduction with the public episode links. Removed the promotional thank-you and repeated podcast introductions from the five chapter integrations; the exact published episode title remains in the sources appendix.
- Appendix G now uses the installed skill throughout: readers bring a question or company context, and the skill reads the bundled book. Removed the conflicting instructions to paste chapters or attach the book.
- More explicit scope for speed and staffing examples, with knowledge updates treated as reviewed releases.
- Vendor assessment that separates onboarding requirements from evidence about how the service operates.
- Build and architecture guidance that considers ownership, maintenance, and composed platforms.
- Clearer distinctions between silence, reported resolution, and observed customer outcomes; calibrated evaluation and context-specific retrieval thresholds.
- Migration plans that include tested recovery capacity.
- Participant descriptions in place of personal names, a lighter fictional-case introduction, and a reader-facing configuration record in the launch inventory.
- Removed misplaced authoring instructions; moved external research links out of the chapter prose into the sources appendix.
- Plain chapter titles in the reading copy, which is now identical to the canonical manuscript.
- One canonical DOCX source and a publishing pipeline that preserves inline links, ordered lists, and unique heading anchors.

Preserved:

- The original v0.2 PDF and DOCX, plus archived Markdown/HTML and existing Shadow Run handouts.

This entry records the prepared edition. It does not claim a public GitHub release or Pages deployment.
