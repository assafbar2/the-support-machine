# Changelog

## Second Edition, revised printing — November 1, 2026

Same edition, same chapters, a sharper book. See the [notes for returning readers](release-notes/v0.4.md).

- Voice pass across all 29 chapters: fewer hedges, shorter openers, and a new field note for every chapter that had a summary instead.
- Three "What I got wrong in the first edition" boxes, in Chapters 8, 9, and 20.
- Chapter 20 is now "Burn the Ships. Keep a Lifeboat."
- Chapter 7's vendor snapshot moved to Appendix H. Chapter 28's method detail moved to the Support AI Evals skill.
- Appendix E is now a field-note index plus the dated observations. Appendix G prints ten starter prompts; all 80 ship with the skill in `references/prompts.md`.
- New subtitle: *How to Deploy and Run AI Customer Support Without Breaking Trust*.

## Second Edition — November 1, 2026

Six new chapters expand the book from 23 to 29 chapters. See the [notes for returning readers](release-notes/v0.3.md) and [chapter reading map](release-notes/v0.3-coverage.md) for where to reread.

- New chapters cover organizational NIMBY behavior, build versus buy, customer agents, action receipts, recovery, and continuing evaluation.
- Expanded operating guidance covers customer promises, the 16% calculation, mature 7/30-day outcome checks, support teams shipping improvements, and a reusable shadow release gate.
- Appendix E collects all 29 chapter field notes and nine dated observations. Appendices F–H connect related repositories, skill setup and 80 practical prompts, and sources by chapter.
- The Prompt Room conversation adds context on self-awareness, sales handoffs, earlier customer signals, debugging judgment, and human handoffs. The book links to the episode on YouTube and Spotify.
- Updated vendor guidance considers lifecycle ownership, acquisition and exit risk, and the documented capabilities of Fin, Pylon, Salesforce, and Plain as of November 1, 2026.
- Companion tools include fourteen templates, the updated book skill, and a standalone Support AI Evals skill with a local cohort calculator.
- Ten black-and-white illustrations explain the operating gates, measurement, continuing evaluation, action recovery, and the midfield model alongside their chapters.
- The complete edition is available in HTML, PDF, DOCX, and Markdown, with matching skill packages. The first edition remains available in its original formats.
