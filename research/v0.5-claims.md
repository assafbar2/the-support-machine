# Claims and sources for the November 20 printing

- **QueueLab is an open-source companion.** The [public source](https://github.com/assafbar2/queuelab) includes the MIT-licensed project, fictional cases, evaluation records, and replay. Third-party checkpoints and adapter binaries are not part of the source release.
- **The reported results are paired local comparisons.** The [run record](https://github.com/assafbar2/queuelab/tree/main/reports/post-training-2026-11-20) preserves both adapters' training records and the before-and-after traces. On the same 60 held-out fictional cases, SmolLM2 scored 0/60 → 53/60 and its unsupported recommendation attempts fell from 36 cases to 7. Falcon scored 0/60 → 3/60 with unsupported recommendations in 39 adapted cases, so it did not pass the specified gain rule.
- **Scope matters.** The holdout contains new synthetic tickets and record wording from known failure families. It wasn't independently practitioner-reviewed. The release does not claim a production result, customer impact, or a causal advantage from model size.
- **Methods and model attribution.** [Hu et al. on LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685), [SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct/tree/31b70e2e869a7173562077fd711b654946d38674), [Falcon-H1-Tiny-Tool-Calling-90M](https://huggingface.co/tiiuae/Falcon-H1-Tiny-Tool-Calling-90M/tree/fc099d791a229464b995fdd2fd24e8ffd2d699e8), and the [interactive trace replay](https://assafbar2.github.io/queuelab/post-training/).
