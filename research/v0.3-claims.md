## Personal accounts and project demonstrations

The panel, talk, and podcast accounts reflect the author's participation and observations. Each participant's example remains a distinct account. The fictional NIMBY cases are teaching scenarios, and the 40% × 40% calculation is illustrative arithmetic.

- [RECEIPT](https://github.com/assafbar2/receipt) demonstrates real worker interruption with simulated providers. It does not establish production reliability or broad crash durability.
- [Promise Ledger](https://github.com/assafbar2/promise-ledger) uses a fictional Northstar account and synthetic examples, with no live CRM or automatic customer sends.
- [Stage Manager / CALL-E](https://github.com/assafbar2/customer-success-voice-signal-hackathon) demonstrates an owner decision and action intent. Downstream live Slack/GitHub execution is unfinished.
- [Suspect Memories](https://github.com/assafbar2/suspect-memories) is a sanitized, deterministic demonstration with one culprit. It does not establish general production accuracy.
- [fcli](https://github.com/assafbar2/fcli) is a related operator utility. Its documented capabilities include test messages, urgent conversations, and volume statistics; the book does not report a fresh execution test.

The seven- and thirty-day evaluation program is a recommended operating practice. Implementations need to establish access, issue linkage, and observation coverage in their own data.
