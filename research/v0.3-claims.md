## Removed or narrowed claims

The edition removes unsupported dashboard precision, a universal silent-churn percentage, fixed AI/human volume shares, a blanket speed multiplier, and a magic retrieval-confidence threshold. It corrects the inference from a vendor questionnaire to hidden human labor, treats training/deletion by data layer, and replaces automatic knowledge promotion with reviewed releases.

The remaining launch story is the author's existing narrative. It is not repackaged as a controlled study. Where outside sources contradict a broad claim, the book narrows the claim rather than using citations as decoration.
